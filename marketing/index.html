Add / Remove — live decisions

Decides itself · checking freshness… override in sheet ↗
Every ad is judged on the result Meta was actually told to buy.
A campaign set to buy landing page views is judged on the cost of a landing page view — not on bookings it was never optimised for. The AD TYPE column tells you which. Bookings are shown on every row regardless, so cheap traffic is never mistaken for a full restaurant.
CPA — £ per booking/order/lead, on every row so the board is comparable. The verdict is still judged on the campaign's own objective — that cost sits in the small print under the CPA. CPC — £ per link click. Owner: Vidya (paid media).
CTR %, LPV % and THRUPLAY % sit in the grey band on the right. They are background information only and do not decide anything — they tell you why a number looks the way it does. Low CTR % = the ad isn't pulling people in (Georgie). Low LPV % = the landing page is losing people who clicked (Vidya). Low ThruPlay % = the video loses people early (Georgie).
Kills sit at the top until they are actually switched off — a KILL on this board is still spending. Once it stops, it moves to the decision history below. Click any row for the agreed date and history.
