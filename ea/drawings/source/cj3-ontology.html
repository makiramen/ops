CJ3 ontology · domain model (PIM) · proof v0.1

Plain English names, only what CJ3 touches. Each class notes the system that holds it (PSM): Kobas for the tab, payments and the cash log; the acquirers and partners for settlements and payouts; the banks for bank lines; Xero for journals and reconciliation. Extends CJ1 / CJ2 (Tab, Payment, Receipt, Discount are the same classes). Not a formal ontology.
