CJ1 ontology · domain model (PIM) · proof v0.2

Plain English names, only what CJ1 touches (Michael's ruling, 5 Nov). Each class notes the Kobas entity (PSM) that holds it, from the extraction (17 entities, 34 edges) or "not in Kobas" where another vendor owns it. Not a formal ontology.
